## Music and sound

Ian: an unfinished descending phrase, initially elegant and detached; do not resolve it into sentimental uplift at the farewell. Ondine: an answering rising interval carried by plucked strings and breath. Tancrede: a sparse pulse that acquires other voices as he earns support. Saint-Orme uses stone resonance, rain on slate, spill water and wood strain; no uninterrupted chapter loop.

Record or synthesize original mechanism Foley with plausible timing. Dialogue must sit above ambience without masking; separate mix channels and skip-state parity remain release requirements. Temp procedural tones, if created, are explicitly scratch and do not count as a finished score.
